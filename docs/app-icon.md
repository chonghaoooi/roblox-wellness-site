# App icon

Source: the +1lean artwork supplied by the owner on 18 October 2026 (1254x1254 PNG, opaque). Master copy: [`public/assets/lean-master.png`](../public/assets/lean-master.png), used unmodified.

Web icon sizes are generated by `scripts/generate-app-icons.mjs` into `public/icons/`: `lean-32.png` (browser tab), `lean-192.png` and `lean-512.png` (app icon), `lean-apple.png` (180px, iPhone Home Screen) and `lean-maskable-512.png`. The maskable version shrinks the artwork to 420px and sits it on a heavily blurred copy of itself, so Android's circle/squircle crop shows matching colours instead of a flat border. Very tight circular masks may trim the corners of the "+1 lean" lettering.

The native step-sync companion under `native/` still uses its earlier icon (`public/assets/fat-to-fit-app.png`).
